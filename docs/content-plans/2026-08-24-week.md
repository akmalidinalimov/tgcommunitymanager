### Tue 25 · AI writes Uzbek. Just not in your voice

Runs off an experiment from 22 Aug. Three generations, same photograph, same palette, only the text
instruction varied. Every one came back correctly spelled with the okina intact, including eleven
dictated words — so the old claim that models garble Uzbek glyphs is dead, and the research brief
that says otherwise is out of date.

What the loose prompt got wrong was **register**: it wrote a headline in the one form the voice
guide bans outright, in a sentence carrying no number, no time and no offer. Correct Uzbek that no
shop owner would ever say.

Note the constraint: lint blocks that verb form, correctly, so the post cannot quote it. The image
carries the evidence and the caption names the suffix. Draft written, 850 chars, lints clean.

### Wed 26 · Nano Banana 2 vs GPT Image 2

The founders' idea, and the audience already does this on its own. Both assets exist, shot 14 Aug:
same Rishtan ceramic painter, same brief, two models.

**Looked at both. Neither is "more realistic" — they are realistic at different things:**

- **Nano Banana 2** — skin and hands. Deep wrinkles, real texture, blue glaze on the fingertip. The
  whole workshop renders: shelves of unglazed jugs read clearly behind her. Bright, documentary,
  press-photograph.
- **GPT Image 2** — light and cloth. The ikat robe is the most convincing thing in either frame.
  Subject emerges from near-black, skin softer and smoother, background swallowed. Cinematic.

Two honest findings worth the post:

1. **More detail is not more believable.** Nano's plate pattern is denser and more symmetrical — and
   that makes it look *printed*. A hand-painted Rishtan plate looks more like GPT's looser one.
2. **The commercial difference is negative space.** GPT left a clear dark third for type. Nano filled
   the frame edge to edge. If a headline is going on it, that decides it before realism does.

Careful with the name: the library once labelled this asset `nano_banana_pro` when the generation
record said `nano_banana_2`. It is **Nano Banana 2**. Two different models.

Needs: the two images composited into one 16:9 so a single Telegram photo carries both.
